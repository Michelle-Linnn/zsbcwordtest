Overview

What we actually do.

We act as agent and representative for local and international companies in commercial and industrial transactions — a role that demands equal parts diplomacy, discipline and deep procedural knowledge.

Our contracting practice serves clients pursuing high-value mandates with government agencies, state-owned enterprises and large corporate buyers.

Our Core Offerings

Every engagement is scoped to your specific objectives — the offerings below are our most requested components.

Bid & Tender Preparation

Compliance matrix, technical response, pricing model and quality-assured submission.
